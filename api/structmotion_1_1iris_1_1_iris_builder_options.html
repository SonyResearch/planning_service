bool motion::iris::IrisBuilderOptions::repair_via_ellipsoid {true}

If true, then the regions will be repaired using hyperplanes guided by the ellipsoid. If false, then the additional hyperplanes will be added by linearization of the robot constraints at the sampled points. The latter is more conservative, but also may finish faster.
